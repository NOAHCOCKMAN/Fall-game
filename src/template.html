Fall Apartment

    
Your apartment, room by room. Live a day in it: keep yourself fed, rested and clean, cook and brew coffee, do the laundry, order delivery to your door, and open packages. Time passes, the sun sets over Mooresville, and there are 38 things to do — cook, text, drive your Corolla to the Harris Teeter or the BP, and meet the neighbors.

  1. Knock on 2B across the hall and say hi to Marcus.

  2. Take the STAIRS door at the end of the hall down to the parking lot.

🅿 Parking lot
    
          
  1. Your red Corolla is parked by the stair door — right-click it to drive.
    2. 
          
  2. W/S gas & reverse, A/D steer, Space brake, left-click horn, right-click radio, E to get out.

  1. Caspian Pl down to Highway 150, straight across onto Perth Rd, and it's up on the right — press M for the map.
    2.

    3. 
          
  3. Say hi to Dana at the register.
